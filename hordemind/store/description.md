## Short description (one line, under 160 characters)

## Full description
**You are the horde.**

In most games you fight the swarm. In Hordemind **you ARE the swarm**. Lead your Queen and a living tide of drones, spitters, bombers and brutes against legendary heroes.

**Hold to swarm**, and every minion charges the hero. Watch for the red danger zones: **pull back** before the hero's attacks land, then flood back in. Fallen minions drop biomass, so dash in with your Queen to collect it and hatch even more.

- 🐜 Command **100+ minions** at once
- 🧬 **Evolve mid-battle** with 18 evolution cards: acid blood, mitosis, neurotoxin, new species… Every hunt is different.
- 👑 **5 Queens**, each with her own brood and special power
- ⚔️ **6 hero bosses**: a Paladin, a Pyromancer, a Ranger, a Frost giant, an Engineer with turrets, and a Necromancer who raises your own dead
- 🏯 **Grow your Hive** between hunts with permanent upgrades
- ⭐ 3 difficulties per hero, 18 stars to collect, and a new **Daily Hunt** every day

Topple every hero and become the Hive Sovereign!

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** WASD or arrow keys to move the Queen · Hold left mouse or Space to SWARM the hero · Release to pull back · E, Shift or right click for the Queen ability · 1 / 2 / 3 to pick evolutions · Esc to pause

**Mobile:** Drag the left side to move · Hold the SWARM button to attack · Tap the ability button for the Queen's power · Tap a card to evolve
