## Full description
**The night never ends. Keep the last ember burning.**

Emberkeep is an idle merge tower defense. Summon wisps around your Ember, then **drag two identical wisps together to merge them** into a stronger, random wisp. Shadows creep in from every direction, wave after endless wave. How long can you hold the night?

- ✨ **9 unique wisps:** Flame, Frost, Storm, Stone, Gold, Venom, Sun, Void and Arc, each with its own power
- 🎲 **Merge strategy:** every merge rolls a random wisp one level higher, up to Lv7, so no two battles play the same
- 🔨 **Workshop:** permanent upgrades that make every battle stronger
- ⚗️ **Laboratory:** real-time research that keeps working while you're away, including Auto-Summon, Auto-Merge and Time Warp
- 🌙 **Offline earnings:** your Keep holds the night and earns coins while you're gone
- 🎁 **Ember Chests** from bosses unlock and level up your wisps
- 🌑 **8 tiers** of ever-darker nights, each with bigger rewards
- 📜 Daily quests, login rewards and achievements

Easy to start, endlessly deep. Come back every day to push the night back further.

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** Click SUMMON (or press Space) to call a random wisp · Drag a wisp onto an identical one (same type and same level) to merge · Click a wisp to see its range · Click a power-up to boost a wisp type for this battle · Esc to pause

**Mobile:** Tap SUMMON · Drag wisps onto their twins to merge · Tap power-ups
