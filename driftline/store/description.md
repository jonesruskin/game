## Full description
**Slide longer, boost harder.**

Driftline is a neon arcade racer you can play with one thumb. Your car accelerates by itself: **hold a turn to drift** through corners, watch the sparks go **blue → orange → purple**, then **let go to fire a boost**. Easy to pick up, very hard to master.

- 🏁 **Career:** 5 cups, 15 tracks and 45 stars to win against 5 rival racers
- 🌙 **Daily Track:** a brand-new track every day, the same for everyone. Earn gold, silver or bronze medals and share your time.
- 👻 **Ghost racing:** your best lap is saved, so race your own ghost and improve
- 🏆 **Leagues** from Rookie to Drift King, with rivals that get smarter as you climb
- 🏎️ **6 cars** with different handling and equal performance, so skill wins races
- 🎨 Paints (even liquid chrome), decals, underglow and tyre smoke, all cosmetic

Are you the next Drift King?

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** ← / → or A / D to steer (the car accelerates by itself) · Hold a turn to drift · Space for an instant drift · ↓ or S to brake · Esc to pause

**Mobile:** Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer · Keep holding through corners to drift · Hold both sides to brake
