## Title
Glint: Gem Cutter Simulator

## Short description (one line, under 160 characters)
Crack rocks, cut dazzling gems with real light physics, and grow a market stall into a royal jewellery empire.

## Full description
**Crack rocks. Cut gems. Get rich.**

Glint is a gem-cutting simulator where every stone is ray-traced in real time. Light really refracts, bounces and splits into rainbow fire inside the gems you cut. A gem's value depends on how well you cut it.

- 🪨 **Crack mystery rocks:** most hold common crystal… but some hide sapphires, emeralds or even a legendary Red Diamond
- 📐 **Plan your cut** inside the rough: bigger means more carats, but dodge the flaws hidden inside
- 🎯 **Find the sweet spot:** turn the dial and watch the value climb. Every mineral has its own perfect angle (quartz is not diamond!)
- ⚙️ **Hold to grind** and release on the perfect meet
- ✨ **Polish to a mirror shine**, then watch your certificate stamp **IDEAL CUT**
- 💰 **Run your jewellery shop:** set your prices, haggle with customers, deliver VIP commissions
- 🏛️ **Grow from Market Stall to Maison Royale:** better tools, showcase lights, apprentices who cut for you, a clerk who sells while you're away
- 🌍 **Buy rough** from the dealer, gamble on mystery rocks, send expeditions to Brazil, Sri Lanka or Colombia, and outbid rivals at the Auction House
- 📖 **Collect 55 gem varieties** from 20 real minerals, each with real optical properties and fun facts
- 📅 **Stone of the Day:** the same mystery rock for everyone. Who can cut it best?

Relaxing, satisfying and surprisingly deep. Learn real gemology while you build your fortune.

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** Click the rock to crack it · Drag the glowing outline to place your cut (drag outside the stone to look around) · Drag the dial to set the angle · Hold the grind button (or Space) and release in the green zone · Rub the stone to polish · Click display cases in your shop to stock them

**Mobile:** Tap to crack · Drag to place the cut · Drag the dial · Hold the grind button · Rub the stone with your finger to polish · Tap cases to stock them
