## Full description
**KO rivals. Steal their powers. Survive the storm.**

Stealstorm is a fast top-down battle royale for 12 players. Every hero starts with two abilities, and when you knock someone out, **their abilities drop as glowing orbs you can steal**. Grab a power you already own and it **upgrades**, up to Level 3. Every match, your build turns into something new: fire walls with grappling hooks, black holes with blinks, frost novas with sentry turrets…

- ⚡ **8 heroes**, each with a unique weapon, passive and ultimate
- 🔮 **22 stealable abilities**, including rare "wild" powers hidden in crates
- ⛈️ A shrinking storm keeps every match tense and short (about 3 minutes)
- 🌿 Bushes for ambushes, crates for loot, and a bounty crown on the top killer
- 🏆 Trophy Road, 7 ranks from Bronze to Stormlord, hero mastery, daily quests and a 30-tier Storm Pass
- 🎨 Skins, trails, KO effects and emotes, all cosmetic. Only skill decides who wins.

Can you become the last one standing?

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** WASD or arrow keys to move · Mouse to aim · Left click to shoot · Q / E for abilities · R, Space or right click for your ultimate · 1 / 2 (or F) to swap in a stolen power · T to emote · Esc to pause

**Mobile:** Drag the left side to move · Tap 🎯 to auto-shoot (drag it to aim) · Tap ability buttons (drag to aim) · Tap a "Replace" button to take a stolen power
