## Full description
**One launch pad. One small agency. The whole universe ahead.**

Starward is a slow-burn space empire builder in the spirit of the great base-building games. Everything runs in real time, even while you're away. Build, research and launch your way out of the cradle: from the first rocket to settlements on Mars, star colonies around real exoplanets, first contact with alien civilisations and expeditions to Andromeda.

- 🚀 **Build your spaceport:** 30+ buildings, every one drawn by hand in code, growing taller and grander as they level up, with a day and night cycle that follows your clock
- 🛰️ **Launch Control:** assemble rockets from the tiny Kestrel to the reusable Leviathan, fly real missions, fill orbit with satellites, and build a space station module by module. Watch every liftoff on the pad.
- 🌕 **Settle the Moon**, then Mars, Ceres and Titan, each with its own sky, ground and colonists to house, feed and employ
- 🪐 **The real Solar System:** 26 worlds with real data, on real orbits. Launch windows open and close as the planets move, and the right day can halve your fuel bill.
- 🌍 **Terraform Mars** in seven stages over weeks of play, and watch the red planet turn blue and green
- ⭐ **Cross to the stars:** 32 real star systems in 3D, from Proxima Centauri to TRAPPIST-1, with their real planets. Light-sail surveys, sleeper arks, ramjets and warp drives; a Dyson Swarm around the Sun; wormhole gates.
- 👽 **Meet seven alien civilisations:** crystalline gardeners from another galaxy, merchants made of light, a machine Synod, a fungal mind, a young ocean people, and the Kr'thul Swarm, which eats worlds. Decipher their languages, trade, ally, and buy their knowledge.
- ⚔️ **Defend humanity:** design a fleet around a weapons triangle, fortify your colonies, intercept raids, and watch every battle replayed. Take the war to the Brood Queen.
- 🌌 **Beyond the galaxy:** wake the ancient relays, send expeditions across the Local Group, build a Matrioshka Brain and a Ringworld, then **Ascend** and begin again stronger
- 📜 **14 chapters, 110 objectives, 101 technologies, 30 achievements,** built to be played for months

No timers you have to pay to skip: dark matter (which finishes timers instantly) is earned by playing, and rewarded ads are always optional.

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** Click a building to upgrade it, or an empty lot to build. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Use the bottom bar to switch between your bases, the orbit view, Launch Control, Research, the Star Map, Contact, Fleet Command and Beyond. Follow the objectives on the left; each pays a reward.
**Mobile:** Tap to select, drag to pan, pinch to zoom. Both portrait and landscape are fully supported.
