# STARWARD: design bible
*From one launch pad on Earth to an empire across galaxies.*

## 1. What makes players stay for years?
Evidence from the genre:
- **OGame** (2002, still running, 100M+ registrations): asynchronous play. *"Many players report that they've played the game for years with only 15–30 minutes of activity daily."* Timers, research, fleets and slow expansion.
- **Clash of Clans:** a base you can *see* grow, builder slots as the scarce resource, upgrades that take seconds at first and days later, and the joy of checking back.
- **Stellaris / Endless Space:** alien civilisations with personalities, diplomacy that matters, and a galaxy that feels alive.
- **CrazyGames today** has small space idlers (First Colony, Space Colony, Tiny Tribes). None is a deep, years-long empire game.

**Starward's formula:**
> CoC's tactile base-building + OGame's patient asynchronous empire + a 4X's alien civilisations, wrapped in a real-science journey that starts at Cape Canaveral-style launch pads and ends at the edge of the observable universe.

Pillars:
1. **Visible progress.** Every upgrade changes how the base looks. Satellites orbit your Earth, stations grow module by module, and terraformed Mars visibly turns blue and green.
2. **Grounded, then wondrous.** It starts as real spaceflight (chemical rockets, real Solar System bodies and data) and climbs into plausible fiction: fusion torches, Alcubierre warp, wormhole gates, Dyson swarms and intergalactic transit.
3. **Many systems, one empire.** Research, building, rockets, satellites, stations, colonies, terraforming, exploration, diplomacy and war all feed one shared economy.
4. **Slow and steady, never stuck.** There is always something finishing soon and something to plan, and offline time always counts.
5. **Aliens with character.** Lower life forms to study, primitive peoples to protect or exploit, advanced civilisations to negotiate with, and hostile empires to fight.

## 3. Economy
A single **empire stockpile** fed by every base. Storage caps come from warehouses, tanks and vaults.

| Resource | Unlocks | Produced by | Spent on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Credits | start | Agency HQ, commercial launches, tourism, trade | everything |
| Metal | start | mines, regolith miners, asteroid harvesters | structures, rockets, ships |
| Fuel | start | refineries, ice extractors, gas skimmers | launches, missions, fleets |
| Science | start | research centres, labs, telescopes, station modules, xeno-studies | research |
| Energy | start (capacity) | solar, fission, fusion, power satellites, Dyson swarms | running buildings (a deficit slows production) |
| Helium-3 | the Moon | regolith harvesters, gas-giant skimmers | fusion reactors and drives |
| Rare elements | asteroids | asteroid mining, Mercury | advanced electronics and hulls |
| Exotic matter | interstellar | Casimir foundries, neutron-star collectors, alien trade | warp drives, wormholes |
| Antimatter | galactic | Penning-trap factories near stars | top drives and weapons |
| Influence | first contact | embassies, cultural exchange | diplomacy |
| Xenodata | first contact | studying life, ruins, artifacts | alien-derived research |
| Dark matter | special | milestones, achievements, rare events, rewarded ads | instant completion, extra crews |

**Scarcity that creates decisions:**
- **Engineering crews** (builders) are the CoC bottleneck. You start with 2 and earn more through research and milestones.
- **Launch windows:** a mission occupies a pad; bigger pads and spaceports allow parallel launches.
- **Research slots:** one at first, more with labs and stations.
- **Energy balance:** every structure draws power, so new reactors gate expansion.

## 4. Bases
Every base is an isometric site with **lots** that unlock with the base's command level. Earth's command centre is **Mission Control**; colonies have a **Colony Hub**. Every building has 10–30 levels, and each level changes how it looks. Base types restyle the ground, sky and building variants: Earth (grass and concrete under a blue sky with a day/night cycle), the Moon (grey regolith, black sky, Earthrise), Mars (rust dust, butterscotch sky), ice moons, gas-giant orbitals, exoplanets.

**Colonists:** off-world bases need people. Population grows toward habitat capacity, and staffing scales output.

## 5. Spaceflight
- **Rockets** are built in the Vehicle Assembly Building: Sounding rocket → Light launcher → Medium → Heavy lifter → Super-heavy reusable → Nuclear shuttle → Fusion torch → Warp cruiser → Gate tender.
- **Missions** (launch control): satellites, probes, crewed flights, station modules, outposts, cargo runs, colony ships, survey fleets and envoys. Missions have real flight times that shrink with propulsion tech, success odds set by reliability, and stakes.
- **Satellites** fill orbital slots (LEO / GEO / Lagrange): comms (+credits), weather (+production), navigation (faster missions), observation, telescopes (+science, reveal targets), solar power (+energy), defence.
- **Space stations** are modular: core, habitat, laboratory, fuel depot, orbital shipyard, observatory, docking ring.
- **Megastructures:** space elevator, orbital ring, mass drivers, Dyson swarm, wormhole gate, matrioshka brain.

## 6. Research
120+ technologies in 10 branches: Rocketry, Engineering, Energy, Computing, Life Sciences, Astronomy and Physics, Colonisation and Terraforming, Military, Xenology and Economy. Early names and descriptions are real science; late ones are clearly labelled speculative. Techs unlock buildings, missions and levels, and grant multipliers.

## 7. Terraforming
A multi-stage project per world: atmosphere, temperature, water, magnetosphere, biosphere. It runs for real days. Habitability climbs, population caps rise and the planet visibly changes in its shader: oceans fill, clouds form, green spreads.

## 8. The galaxy
Real nearby stars (Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star, Wolf 359, Sirius, Epsilon Eridani, Tau Ceti, TRAPPIST-1…) then a seeded procedural Milky Way of thousands of systems. Other galaxies follow (Magellanic Clouds, Andromeda, Triangulum, Sombrero…). Exploration: probes reveal systems; surveys reveal worlds, resources, life and ruins.

## 9. Aliens
**Tiers of life:** microbial → flora and fauna (lower life forms) → tribal (low intelligence) → industrial → spacefaring → ascended.

**Handcrafted civilisations with story arcs:**
- **Veyari:** ancient crystalline "gardeners"; benevolent but testing; they teach those who show restraint.
- **Kr'thul Swarm:** a hive of insectoids that consumes worlds; the great hostile threat.
- **Lumen Concord:** beings of plasma and light; mercantile; traders of exotic matter.
- **Oshoru:** an aquatic people before their industrial age; protect, uplift or exploit them.
- **Synod of Iron:** a machine civilisation that negotiates on data and can become ally or nemesis.
- **Mycelar:** a fungal network mind; slow and peaceful; offers biotech for terraforming.
- **The Hollow:** an extinct precursor race whose ruins and artifacts hold xenotech.
- **Ascendants:** transcendent beings beyond Andromeda; the endgame mystery.

Procedurally generated minor species fill the rest of the galaxy (portraits, names, temperaments, homeworlds).

**Diplomacy:** relations from −100 to +100; gifts, trade, research pacts, treaties, alliances, tribute, war. Advanced civilisations trade *knowledge* (unique techs) for favours, resources or quests.

**Conflict:** warship classes (corvette to titan), planetary defences and shields, round-based battles with visual replays, raids on colonies, and conquest or liberation of worlds.

## 10. Rhythm and fair monetisation
- Timers run from seconds early to days late. Offline progress is complete: timers finish and production accrues up to storage caps.
- **Dark matter** (earned, never bought) finishes timers instantly. Rewarded ads (optional) skip 30 minutes on one timer (a few per day) or grant a dark-matter bonus.
- Midgame ads only at natural breaks (returning to the base after a completed mission), spaced out.

## 11. Build plan (each part committed when done)
1. **Foundation & Earth:** engine (state, saves, offline time, economy, timers), the isometric Earth base, buildings and upgrades, early research, objectives.
2. **Orbit & Moon:** rockets, launch control, launches, satellites, the space station, a WebGL planet view, the lunar outpost.
3. **The Solar System:** every major body with real data, a system map, colonies, colonists, asteroid mining, terraforming.
4. **Interstellar:** galaxy map, real nearby stars plus a procedural Milky Way, interstellar drives, megastructures.
5. **Contact:** alien species (handcrafted plus procedural), first contact, xenology, diplomacy.
6. **Conflict:** fleets, defences, raids, battles, conquest.
7. **Beyond:** intergalactic travel, endgame megaprojects, Ascension (prestige), achievements, codex.
8. **Polish & ship:** audio, tutorial, balance sim covering months of play, store assets, docs.
