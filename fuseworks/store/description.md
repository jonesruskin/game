## Full description
**One spark. A hundred explosions.**

Fuseworks is a fireworks chain-reaction roguelike. Every firework in the sky has a reach. Light one and it bursts, lighting every firework it touches, which light the next, and the next… Set it up right and the entire sky goes off at once: a **GRAND FINALE**.

Every burst adds **Sparks** and **Heat**. When the chain ends, **Sparks × Heat** is your score. Beat the crowd's target to clear the act, then spend your earnings at the Bazaar on **Catalysts**: game-changing perks that bend the rules of fire.

- 🎆 **11 firework types:** Peony, Willow, Palm, Crossette, Crackle, Ring, Glitter, Strobe, Comet, Spider and Crown, each with its own reach and personality
- 🃏 **52 Catalysts** to discover: from *Kindling* to *Mirror Image*, *Supernova* and the legendary *Big Bang*
- ✋ **Plan your chain:** drag fireworks into reach before you light them, then watch the cascade
- 🔥 **Multiply everything:** formulas level up your favourite fireworks, rites dye, gild and twin them, blueprints rewrite the show
- 👑 **8 nights, 24 acts, 14 bosses** with twisted rules: The Gale, The Critic, The Fog, The Eclipse…
- 🌈 **Combos:** colour streaks, Rainbow bonuses, Grand Finales
- 📅 **Daily Show:** the same skies and shops for everyone, every day. Compare your score with friends.
- ♾️ **Endless mode,** 5 heat levels, 6 crates to unlock, 20 achievements and a full collection to complete
- 🎨 **Pyro Locker:** sky themes, rocket trails and heart, star or smiley-shaped fireworks, all earned by playing

Easy to start (just tap a firework), deep enough to break: every run is a new build.

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** Click a firework to light it · Drag a firework to move it into reach (limited Moves per act) · Hover a firework to preview its reach · Drag catalysts to reorder them (left → right) · Space lights the hovered firework · Esc to pause

**Mobile:** Tap a firework to preview its reach, tap it again to light it · Drag to move it · Tap catalysts for details
