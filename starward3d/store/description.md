## Full description
**A floating island in orbit. A crew of robots. The whole sky to claim.**

Starward 3D is a cosy, come-back-daily colony builder in full 3D. Lay out your island however you like, raise a crew of robots and AIs who earn while you're away, and grow from one bay above Earth to bases on the Moon, Mars and an ancient alien vault.

- 🤖 **Collect 114 robots and AIs:** 22 elements, 7 rarities from Standard to Singularity, every one built in 3D with its own look. Feed them, level them, post them as helpers to speed up your buildings.
- 🧬 **Fuse new species:** pair two bots in the Fusion Lab and boot the core that comes out. Rare combinations make rare bots, and the Botdex remembers every one you find.
- 🏝️ **Build it your way:** free placement on a floating island with Earth turning below. Bays, Fabricators, the Assembly Bay, the Fusion Lab, the Arena, a Launch Pad and 17 decorations, including a golden statue of your favourite bot and a neon sign with your colony's name.
- 🎨 **Make it yours:** 49 styles to earn: paint schemes, banners and emblems, and island turfs from Sakura Terrace to Neon Grid. Every island can wear its own. Then make a colony card and share it.
- 🎁 **Something new every day:** a 28-day login calendar that never resets, three daily missions, supply drops falling from orbit, a free daily core spin and 16 achievements with 64 tiers to climb.
- ⚔️ **The Arena:** 3v3 turn-based battles with element matchups, leagues and weekly seasons. Defend your colony from the Kr'thul Swarm and capture its reprogrammed drones.
- 🚀 **Road to the stars:** crew rockets with your bots and fly expeditions to ten real worlds of the Solar System, then to five real stars. Meet five alien civilisations and bring home their bots.
- 🌕 **New islands:** found Tranquility Base on the Moon, Bradbury Landing on Mars, and the Precursor Vault, where relic crystals wake Relic AIs that earn Iridium.
- 🏆 **Weekly events:** a new theme every Monday with an exclusive bot at the top of the reward track.

Everything keeps running while you're away. Dark Matter, which finishes timers, is earned by playing; ads are always optional.

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** Click a building to open it, click a bubble to collect. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag to rotate the view. Use the bottom bar for the Shop, your Crew, the Botdex, the Arena, Space and Style. Follow the mission card on the left; each pays a reward.
**Mobile:** Tap to select and collect, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to rotate. Portrait and landscape are both supported.
