## Full description
**One launch pad on a coastal plain. Everything else is up to you.**

Helioscend is a come-back-daily space empire builder in full 3D. Build a spaceport the way you like it, launch rockets you designed, settle real worlds, find alien life and grow a civilisation measured on the Kardashev scale, from a planet's power to a galaxy's.

- 🚀 **A real space programme:** design rockets part by part, fly test flights, put satellites in orbit and build a space station above Earth.
- 🌍 **Bases on real worlds:** the Moon, Mars, Ceres, Titan and a cloud city floating over Venus, each with its own sky, ground and rules. Your people live in domes and in tunnels your robots dig, and they need water, air and food: algae vats, greenhouses and deep farms, or supply runs from Earth. Watch Mars go from red dust to rivers and green valleys, stage by stage, then terraform worlds around other stars.
- 🧭 **Land to explore and claim:** choose where each colony sets down (Shackleton's ice, Marius Hills' lava tubes, Hellas's thick air), send rovers to find ore seams, ice, helium-3 and geothermal vents, and claim the land plot by plot. Build on a deposit and it works harder; build in shelter and storms pass you by.
- 🤖 **Robots that work for you:** collect robot cores, activate and fuse them, and post each one where its elements fit. The Robot Registry remembers every species.
- 🧬 **The Life Ladder:** look for life where scientists really look, under Mars's ice, in Venus's clouds, in the geysers of Enceladus and the methane seas of Titan, then on worlds around other stars. Study it and it pays you back for ever. Eight rungs, from microbes to leviathans drifting in Jupiter's clouds to civilisations older than our species.
- 👽 **First contact:** meet seven civilisations at real stars. Decipher their languages, trade, sign pacts and alliances, uplift a young species, and decide what to do about the Swarm.
- ⚔️ **Daily Orbital Trials:** lay out your defences grid-based and send your robots against other players' bases. Every battle is replayable and the outcome is the same every time it is watched.
- 🛡️ **Defend what you build:** Swarm landings on Earth and fleet battles in space over your colonies, with forecasts that show exactly what would happen if they landed now.
- ✨ **To the stars and beyond:** a real star map, colonies under other suns, a Dyson swarm around the Sun, wormhole gates, expeditions to Andromeda and the Far Shore, and Ascension when you get there.
- 🤖 **Aova, your programme's AI:** it never plays for you. Queue research and upgrades and it carries them out, and later it moves food between your colonies.
- 🎨 **Make it yours:** paint schemes, banners and emblems to earn, ten decorations, and a colony card to share.
- 🎁 **Something every day:** a login calendar that never resets, daily missions, supply capsules falling from the sky, a free daily core spin and weekly events.

Everything keeps running while you are away. Dark matter, which finishes timers, is earned by playing; ads are always optional.

## Controls / instructions
**Desktop:** Click a building to open it, click a bubble to collect. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag to rotate. The bottom bar has Build, Land (survey and claim plots), Research, Robots, Launch, Orbit, the Map and your Goals; the right-hand rail has the daily rewards, Contact, Life, Defence and Orbital Trials; 🎨 opens the Style Studio. Follow the goal card: every goal pays a reward.
**Mobile:** Tap to select and collect, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to rotate. Portrait and landscape are both supported.
